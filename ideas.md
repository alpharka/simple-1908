# MenikahSudahDekat — Simple Minimalist

## Chosen Direction: Quiet Editorial Card

The supplied Simple Minimalist blueprint is the design ground truth. The invitation will feel like a carefully composed personal wedding card: calm, warm, couple-led, and intentionally spare. It will not introduce unrequested navigation, ornamental motifs, saturated color, gradients, dense UI, or additional information architecture.

### Design Movement

Contemporary **editorial stationery** with the restraint of refined print invitations: quiet typography, white space, natural portrait photography, and thin rules instead of decorative framing.

### Core Principles

1. **Photography before ornament:** the couple and supplied imagery remain the primary visual focus.
2. **Whitespace as structure:** generous breathing room creates the story rhythm rather than decorative separators.
3. **Quiet hierarchy:** serif display moments and a clean sans-serif body establish information clarity without visual noise.
4. **One uninterrupted story:** every section continues the same vertical editorial composition with no competing navigation.

### Color Philosophy

Warm off-white surfaces create intimacy without a stark clinical white. Deep charcoal anchors accessible reading contrast; muted warm gray supports secondary metadata; a dusty olive-neutral accent is reserved for focus, active states, and fine detail. No saturated colors, gradients, or metallic effects are used.

### Layout Paradigm

A continuous, mobile-first **portrait letter** unfolds from a full-screen cover into narrow reading columns and wider media moments. Major content is centered where ceremonial; longer text moves left only for comfortable reading. Cards are editorial information blocks, not dashboard tiles.

### Signature Elements

1. A small uppercase “Wedding Invitation” overline paired with a refined serif name treatment.
2. Hairline rules that provide tempo between related pieces of information.
3. Offset editorial photo crops with clear negative space and subtle caption labels.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interaction stays functional and quiet. The cover has one opening action; map, calendar, RSVP, gallery, video, gift-copy, and wish interactions are obvious, keyboard reachable, and lightly responsive. Buttons compress gently when pressed and retain a visible focus ring.

### Animation

Sections enter with opacity and short vertical translation only. Hero media receives the slowest fade and scale treatment; supporting information uses modest reveal timing. All motion is disabled or simplified when reduced motion is requested.

### Typography System

Cormorant Garamond provides the refined editorial serif for names and ceremonial headings. DM Sans provides clear body copy, labels, metadata, and controls. Small labels use uppercase with increased tracking; body copy has generous line-height; names are never oversized beyond mobile readability.

### Brand Essence

**An intimate digital wedding card for couples who want their story and guest information to feel composed, personal, and unhurried.**

Personality: **refined, sincere, spacious**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are spare and ceremonial; calls to action are direct and respectful; microcopy is helpful without promotional filler.

- “Kami mengundang Anda untuk hadir di hari bahagia kami.”
- “Simpan tanggal dan temukan perjalanan kami.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a single abstract ribbon loop representing two paths meeting; it contains no text. The wordmark uses spaced small caps paired with the editorial serif name treatment.

### Signature Brand Color

**Dried Olive — #7C806A.** A quiet neutral-green accent that brings natural warmth to the off-white and charcoal foundation.

## Style Decisions

- Supplied people photography is used for the cover, couple, stories, and gallery; no AI-generated people or couple imagery is permitted.
- The opening cover contains no navigation and keeps “Buka Undangan” as the one primary action.
- Styling may use only restrained surface detail that does not read as a decorative pattern or compete with faces.
- **Photography rule:** supplied people imagery is visually harmonized with a consistent warm editorial treatment and natural cropping; no unrelated media sources are added.
- **Voice rule:** interface labels and section headings use spare, ceremonial Indonesian; supplied data values remain untouched.
- **Accent rule:** Dried Olive `#7C806A` recurs only in fine rules, labels, focus states, and small icons—not as a decorative color field.
